Research Background

Cultural Heritage Crisis

Studies reveal a sharp decline in local cultural awareness among Filipino youth. Tabiolo (2025) reported that only 3 in 10 students can identify native folklore creatures, while overwhelmingly recognizing Greek, Norse, and Japanese mythological icons.

🎯 Thesis Objective

Design, develop, and evaluate a mobile AR game that reconnects students with indigenous Philippine folklore through markerless exploration.

⚙️ System Requirements

Platform: Android 8.0+ (Oreo)
AR Stack: AR Foundation & ARCore
Sensors: Camera, GPS, Compass
Storage: ~110 MB free space

📊 Validation Results

Learning Impact: +78.6% knowledge recall gain.

Pillar 1: Exploration

Geospatial Radar & Circadian Cycle

Real-world outdoor GPS is seamlessly blended with an indoor dead-reckoning pedometer and digital compass rose, enabling students to explore classrooms, campus quads, and sports facilities.

🧭 Haversine GPS Distance

Calculates spherical distances between the player's mobile coordinates and spirit spawn nodes within a 45m perimeter.

🌙 Circadian Cycle Engine

Spawns dynamically adapt to real-world time (DateTime.Now.Hour):
• Diurnal (Day: 6 AM–6 PM): Diwata, Nuno, Bungisngis.
• Nocturnal (Night: 6 PM–6 AM): Tikbalang, Kapre, Batibat, Aswang.

⚡ 6-Meter Proximity Alert

Approaching within 6.0m triggers golden haptic vibration, transitioning seamlessly into the AR encounter.

Pillar 2: Surface Detection • ARCore horizontal & vertical plane raycasting without artificial paper markers.

Pillar 3 & 4: Capture & Codex

Sacred Agimat & Babaylan Quiz

Foreign capturing balls are replaced by the consecrated Philippine *Anting-Anting* talisman. Capturing commits the spirit to the 64-bit codex bitmask.

🧿 Sacred Agimat Physics

Players throw parabolic agimat talismans calculated with real-world gravity and collision hitboxes.

📜 Indigenous Lore Codex

Unlocked entries display cultural narratives, regional habitats, and traditional countermeasures (e.g. turning shirts inside out for Tikbalang).

✨ Babaylan Lore Quiz

5-Question randomized cultural quiz tests lore retention. Scoring 60%+ awards bonus EXP, Ginto currency, and achievement badges.

OFFLINE-FIRST DESIGN: Full gameplay loop runs with zero internet connection. Optional Supabase cloud account syncs progress when online.
