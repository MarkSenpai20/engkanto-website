THE PROBLEM & ACADEMIC RATIONALE

Why Eng-Kanto Matters to Philippine Cultural Heritage

Academic studies have documented an alarming disconnect between contemporary Filipino youth and native cultural mythology.

📉

The Cultural Disconnect

Field research (Tabiolo, 2025) found that only 3 out of 10 students in surveyed schools could name more than two indigenous mythical beings, while overwhelmingly recognizing Greek, Norse, and Japanese figures.

📖

Traditional Limitations

Textbook-based lectures fail to sustain attention among digital natives. Without experiential media, rich folk narratives from Ilocos, Visayas, Bicol, and Tagalog regions remain inaccessible.

✨

The AR & Gamification Solution

By transforming students into active *Agimat Hunters* walking their physical surroundings, learning shifts from passive reading to visceral augmented reality encounters and cultural retention.

CORE GAME SYSTEMS

The 4-Pillar Exploration & Learning Loop

Engineered for authentic folklore discovery without requiring expensive wearable headsets or external sensors.

01
🧭

Geospatial Radar & Circadian Spawning

Spherical Haversine GPS mathematics calculate real-world distance. Built-in compass heading and indoor pedometer dead-reckoning let students navigate both open school grounds and indoor classrooms.

  • Diurnal Spawns: Diwata, Nuno, Bungisngis (6 AM – 6 PM)
  • Nocturnal Spawns: Tikbalang, Kapre, Batibat (6 PM – 6 AM)
  • 6.0-Meter Proximity Encounter Trigger
02
📱

Markerless AR Surface Anchoring

Powered by Unity AR Foundation and Google ARCore. Automatically detects horizontal floor planes and vertical surfaces without requiring artificial QR image targets.

  • Automatic floor plane detection
  • Dynamic camera billboard rotation
  • Seamless transition from 2D Radar to AR View
03
🧿

Folklore Scale & Sacred Agimat

Every creature is calibrated to its true folklore height in meters: from the towering 3.20m *Bakunawa* sea dragon down to the 0.45m *Nuno sa Punso* earthen mound spirit. Players capture spirits by casting the consecrated Philippine *Agimat* talisman.

  • True folklore heights in real meters
  • Physics-driven trajectory throw mechanics
  • Authentic Anting-Anting cultural talisman
04
📜

Cultural Codex & Babaylan Quiz

Capturing a spirit permanently commits its index to the 64-bit Bestiary bitmask. Unlocked entries reveal regional origins, mythological lore, and traditional countermeasures, followed by the randomized 5-question Babaylan Lore Quiz.

  • 64-Bit bitmask persistent progression
  • Bilingual lore narratives (English / Filipino)
  • Rewards Ginto currency & level progression
